Independent review driver:
  Lynxer/reproducer/lynxer_public_forgery.c
  The driver was written independently from the manuscript description. It:
    * creates a victim key pair through sig_keygen;
    * erases the secret-key buffer;
    * resets the global DRNG to a separate public attacker seed;
    * derives C2 from the public OWF input;
    * constructs w = (C2, 0, S2(L0(C2)+C1));
    * confirms Lynx(C2) differs from the victim target;
    * calls the submitted public prover and unmodified sig_verify;
    * confirms the accepted signature rejects on a changed message.
