DARTS-128 17-million-signature one-instance recovery and forgery
Date: 2026-09-26

Scope
  One deterministic DARTS-128 target key.
  The fixed completion execution consumed only the public key, decoded public
  signature fields, and fixed attack parameters. It did not read true labels
  or hidden branch counters. The classifier widths, support counts, and
  equation count had nevertheless been selected during exploration that used
  target-secret diagnostics. This is not an out-of-sample experiment and does
  not estimate success probability over randomized key generation.

Claim terminology
  Exact ternary-secret recovery followed by equivalent signing-key
  construction. All 1,536 coefficients of (s0,s1,e) were recovered exactly.
  The original seed K was not recovered; a fresh K was used for the accepted
  forgery. The result is a successful EUF-CMA-style experiment for one fixed
  key, not by itself a practical EUF-CMA break of DARTS-128 as a scheme.

Estimator interpretation
  For each response polynomial, the exact identity is
    theta = G^-1 sum(C^T y) + G^-1 [sum(epsilon C^T C)] s.
  Replacing the second matrix by E[epsilon] G and the first term by zero is a
  heuristic requiring accepted-transcript correlation assumptions that have
  not been proved. Post-hoc 17M regression slopes were -0.04159580 for s0 and
  -0.04158852 for s1, versus empirical mean epsilon=-0.11704929.

Current limitation
