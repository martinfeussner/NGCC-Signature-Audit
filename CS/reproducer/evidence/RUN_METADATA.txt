The calibration budget and every parameter of the public candidate ladder were
fixed before refinement collection.  The collector paused after each 100,000
signatures from worker 103.  Collection stopped immediately after the normal
and poisoned-diagnostic builds both accepted at 1,300,000 refinement
signatures.  Worker 103 therefore intentionally ended at its paused 300,000
checkpoint; status 8 in its timing file records EOF at that checkpoint gate,
after the state had been written.

The managed execution environment scheduled the signing processes below the
host's full four-core capacity.  Aggregate user CPU and observed elapsed time
are therefore both reported.  The elapsed total also includes the deliberate
pauses used to test the 1.1M, 1.2M, and 1.3M refinement checkpoints.

Evidence
  public_ladder.txt records rejection at every 100,000-signature refinement
  checkpoint from 100,000 through 1,200,000 and acceptance at 1,300,000.
  The poison-control ladder replaces every secret-dependent diagnostic truth
  array with unrelated values and has the same public stopping point, completed
  equivalent key, and accepted forgery.
  State files are raw little-endian x86-64 records containing IEEE-754 doubles.
